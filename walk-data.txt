The derived walking database is included under that license in walk-network.js.
Source: OSM API 0.6 map extracts. Bounds are supplied in walkNetwork.bounds
as west, south, east, north (WGS84). Ways are clipped to the union of the
extracts. Coordinates in ways and crossings are ordered latitude, longitude.
Thematic selection is based on highway, access, foot, bridge and tunnel tags.
Expressways and general streets give location context. Only mapped pedestrian
ways/steps and explicitly walkable paths are emphasized as pedestrian links.
The named management-line greenway is a modern reference feature, not a
survey of the 1994 boundary. No real-time closures or turn routing are supplied.

Additional geometry in landscape.js is derived from OSM and licensed under ODbL 1.0.
Historical street names are a curated subset. Current centerlines and water outlines
are geographic references. Selected Meilin patrol corridors follow named sections
of Erxian Road and mapped stone-paved non-bridge sections of the management-line
greenway. They are generalized historical references, not a survey of the fence.
